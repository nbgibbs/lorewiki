After escaping the burning room through the window the party found cover in an abandoned building while [[Shalók]] returned to attempt to look for [[Dr. V]], but he only maneged to find a shard of [[Mysterium]] that exploded in his face temporarily blinding him. Making matters worse, when some guards came looking he spooked like a deer and ran face first into a wall knocking him out cold.

[[Rowan]] managed to bring Shallok back to the others and overheard they were sending some special team to deal with whoever blew up this government building. Then after some debating on how to get out of town it was decided that we would hop a train in the freight station across town. However before we made it far we were discovered and a chase ensued with these brightly colored special forces catching up just before we made it to the train station.

In the fight Rowan took a fatal crossbow bolt, but before he died, he took his now blood soaked deck of enchanted cards and threw them into the brightly colored guards until they had all dropped. His last thoughts of his fledgling team of misfits.

The misfits, now with 2 bodies, one unconscious and one dead, snuck aboard the first box car they found and tried to get what rest they could before they arrived wherever they were going.
