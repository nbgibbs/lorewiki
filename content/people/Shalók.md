Covered from head to foot in bark, moss, leaves and twigs. He wears a mask that is also covered in bark and with gnarled branches like roots reaching up like the antlers of a stag. Bright green eyes are visible through the only holes in the mask.
His face has been seen by other members of the party on 2 occasions. Both were when his mask needed to be removed in order to give him a potion of healing. First it was [[Partitio]] fighting [[2b]] then [[Rowan]] during an encounter with [[Ember Voss]].
Partitio and Rowan are the only known people to have seen his face behind the mask.
He has light brown skin and dark brown hair and strikingly a handful of green vanes that branch from his eyes in a crystalline pattern. During [[session 4]] while looking for [[Dr. V]] a piece of [[Mysterium]] exploded temporarily blinding him and leaving a red lighting shaped scar on his mask.
