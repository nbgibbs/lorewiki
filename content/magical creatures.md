# Vyre-lum

Small fairy like. ethereal. curved tail luminous tail over head. jump in trees. sign of good luck. monkey angler fish combo. a tad smaller than a soda can. Live in Vyre trees.


>While exploring the Verdant Forest I discovered a fascinating species of tree. I call it a Vyre tree. During the daytime it's bulb-like fruit glow very faintly. During the night if one is brave enough to tramp through the undergrowth the glow is extremely noticeable and comforting.
>
>I made an incredible discovery about the Vyre trees that I have written about before! There is a small and elusive creature (I call them Vyre-lums) that live in these trees!
>
These creatures are about the size of a copper cup. They use their tiny hands to scamper and hop from tree to tree. Their curved tails reach past their own heads and glow just like the luminous fruit pods contained within each Vyre flower. Some say that they are good luck to be seen bcause of how elusive. 
>
The light of the trees attracts insects which also happens to be a large part of their diet. I hypothesize that this is possibly a symbiotic relationship between the trees and Vyre-lums. It is possible that some of the insects I have yet to document are harmful to the Vyre tree. 

\- [[Johnny the Explorer]]

huge predator. some time a problem to the city. can't be contained. gryphon like. 2 legs. shorter wingspan to fly between the large tree. 

>While traversing the forest, I encountered a truly magnificent creature known as the Windterror, or the "Final Gale." This colossal griffin-like being boasts four majestic wings that stretch wide, catching the slightest breeze with an elegance that belies its fearsome reputation. Its sleek, feathered body glistens like polished obsidian, a striking contrast to the rocky terrain, and its razor-sharp talons glint menacingly in the light, ready to strike at a moment's notice.
>
The Windterror is a masterful predator, hunting the largest mammals of the land, including the fearsome [[Titanoboa]] and powerful bears. With a wingspan that can overshadow the tallest trees, it soars effortlessly through the skies, its piercing eyes scanning the ground for any sign of movement. The powerful beats of its wings create an eerie silence in the air, and those fortunate—or unfortunate—enough to witness its hunting prowess speak of its terrifying grace. Legends say that the Windterror is a harbinger of storms, leaving a chilling breeze in its wake as it descends to claim its next prey, embodying the relentless fury of nature itself.

# Hunyip
Hummingbird-like creatures, but their mouths are more like that of a butterfly. They are able to fly any direction at incredible speeds, particularly aided by their five eyes. The eyes are placed around their head such that they're able to see side-to-side at all times, and straight up above them. They're well known for stealing trinkets and random non-eye-catching things. They use them as cover, attached between their scales (because feathers or fur would be rather uncomfortable) mainly because without cover, their scales are iridescent.  Based on the color of their scales, they will have a different precious gemstone in their core, only able to be extracted by taking off their scales one by one and carved out of them while they're alive. If extracted while they are dead, the structure of the gemstone will collapse in on itself and become graphite (think diamonds when they leave a volcano).
They are fairly solitary creatures and leave their young to find for themselves, hidden among the tree canopy. (And, hey since they have butterfly mouths) Their eggs are laid in the best food storage their mother is able to hoard away. They mainly consume nectar and insects, though some have been reported to carry away small mammals

Calls out to females with a slight "yip" sound, but moves in a pattern between each call so only one of their kind (in theory) would know where to go to find the male.
